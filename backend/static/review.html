One question. One handoff. One complete record.

OrderProof helps small businesses answer from approved policies, continue with a person, and preserve what both sides said.

Current AI limitation: the AWS account’s Bedrock token quota blocks live generation. The app saves the message and offers human support. The public walkthrough uses labeled fictional responses. We do not present those as real AI results.

Explore without signing in

Open the application and choose Explore the demo. Inspect company knowledge, join a conversation, reply, close it, and open Conversation records. This walkthrough is fictional and creates no protected archive.

Verify the real workflow

  1. Sign in to a real workspace and add an approved source: “Unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Contact support before sending a return.”
  2. Open Preview widget in a separate browser context. Consent to recording and ask: “Can I return an unused item after 20 days?”
  3. When model access is available, inspect the answer’s source reference. While quota is blocked, verify the explicit fallback instead.
  4. Choose Talk to a person. In the owner inbox, join and reply. The customer should see the reply in the same thread.
  5. Close the conversation. Verify the record and protected archive, then export the JSON transcript. Closed records cannot receive further messages.

What the record proves

The hash chain and archive check detect inconsistencies in stored events and the sealed transcript. They do not independently prove identity or truth. S3 governance retention is bypassable by appropriately privileged AWS administrators.

Access during judging

Arrange reviewer access before relying on an existing workspace for judging; the public fictional walkthrough does not expire.
