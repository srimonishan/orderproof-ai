# Independent owner invitation

Draft only. No invitation has been sent and no owner has agreed to participate.

## Invitation to send personally

Hi [name], I’m building OrderProof, a small-business support widget that preserves the conversation when customers move from automated support to a person. Would you be willing to test it with me for 20 minutes using your own non-sensitive business policies?

I want honest feedback, including anything that is confusing, slower, or not useful. We would compare a few support tasks with your current process, including finding an earlier promise and exporting a record. There is no payment or subscription commitment. Participation and any follow-up are optional.

The AI service is currently blocked by an AWS account quota, so the initial session would evaluate human handoff and records only. I will not describe that as an AI test. We can evaluate actual AI answers separately if access is restored.

With your permission, I would record task durations, successes and failures, and your feedback. I would not publish your name, customer content, or quotations without separate approval. Would [date/time] work?

## First session — 20 minutes

1. Confirm the owner agrees to participate and explain retention and publication permissions (3 minutes).
2. Ask how they currently answer policy questions, hand off requests, and find earlier promises (3 minutes).
3. Observe one baseline task and a comparable OrderProof task, including a human reply and record retrieval/export (8 minutes).
4. Ask what worked, what failed, whether they would return, and whether the actual displayed plan price is acceptable (4 minutes).
5. Agree to an optional follow-up date and whether anonymized observations may be shared (2 minutes).

Use the observation templates in this directory. Record the actual small sample; do not call it representative. Repeat with two additional independent owners if available, without inventing missing participation. Three owners is a research target, not an official hackathon requirement.

Do not coach positive ratings or reward favorable quotations. On follow-up, distinguish voluntary return use from another requested test. A founder demonstration is not an independent owner session; a stated willingness to pay is not revenue.
