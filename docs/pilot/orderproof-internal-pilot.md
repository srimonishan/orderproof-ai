# OrderProof internal usability pilot

Participant organization named by the founder: OrderProof.
Status: **internal pilot prepared; observations not yet collected**.

This is founder testing of the product itself, not an independent customer pilot. Results may support usability findings, but must not be described as customer demand, paid adoption, market validation, or product-market fit. Do not use the real-business observation report to imply otherwise.

## Tasks

Record actual start/end times, success/failure, problems encountered, and proposed fixes for each task. Use fictional customer information. Label all resulting conversations synthetic.

1. Create or open a workspace and approve a returns policy.
2. Open its real customer widget and ask about that policy. Record whether the response is a genuine Bedrock answer or the unavailable fallback. Never substitute a prepared demo response.
3. Request a person, join from the owner inbox, and send a reply. Check the customer receives it.
4. Close the conversation, verify the archive, and export the transcript.
5. Try adding a message after closure; confirm the app rejects it.
6. Find a specific earlier statement in the exported record.
7. Try the customer flow on a phone-sized viewport and record any confusing controls.
