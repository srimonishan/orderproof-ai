# Official Rules review

Verified September 20, 2026 by opening the public Rules tab in Chromium; page last updated September 18, 2026.

Source: https://builder.aws.com/build/hackathons/e83e84e5-4f4c-383b-bbe9-4a15ac195d55/zero-to-shipped?tab=rules

- Sri Lanka is not among the listed excluded countries. Participant self-reports residence there and age 19; independent finalist verification remains AWS’s responsibility.
- Minimum age is 18. Amazon employee, immediate-family and household exclusions and the one-entry rule still require participant confirmation.
- Required: public live AWS app, documented agent-console connection, published Builder Center project with development process and AWS/agent use, category and track, and an original previously unpublished application.
- The ship gate requires public reachability for both judges and AI scoring, plus connection proof.
- Each criterion carries 25%: Technical Innovation & Originality, Implementation Quality, Community/Market Impact, Creativity & Storytelling. The Rules take priority over differing About-page wording.
- The top 100 advance to human judging; five winners are selected across categories. No guaranteed or separately ranked first-place award is established.
- Deadline: October 2, 2026, 11:59 p.m. Pacific. Keep the deployed app available throughout evaluation.

This is a factual requirements checklist, not a guarantee of organizer acceptance. The official Rules govern the entry.
